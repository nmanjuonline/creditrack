## Overall Impression

CrediTrack is an exceptionally well-crafted, focused, and coherent web application. It solves complex financial timing calculations (45–52 day interest-free runway) and delivers the answer within seconds. The tactile mobile stack and clean desktop multi-column grid demonstrate strong platform respect. Addressing form disclosure in the modal and keyboard shortcuts will complete the experience.

## What's Working

1. **Deterministic Float Runway & Urgency Hierarchy:** The "Float Runway" badge (`48 DAYS`) and color-coded cut/due badges give instant operational clarity, with urgent payment deadlines (<= 3 days) immediately catching the eye.
2. **Tactile Mobile Deck & Clean Desktop Divergence:** Mobile features a 1:1 touch-tracked card carousel, while desktop screens (`>= 768px`) strictly enforce a responsive multi-column grid, avoiding layout clutter.
3. **Local-First Privacy Architecture:** Transparent UX guarantees peace of mind—never asking for sensitive 16-digit card numbers, CVVs, expiration dates, or bank credentials.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Form Field Overload in Add/Edit Card Modal**
  - **Why it matters:** The modal presents 10 fields at once, creating unnecessary cognitive friction and visual intimidation for users who simply want to track a card's billing date.
  - **Fix:** Introduce progressive disclosure: keep Bank, Card Model, Statement Day, Grace Period, and Auto-Fetch upfront; collapse secondary attributes (Nickname, Credit Limit, Fixed Due Day, Color Finish, Notes) behind a sleek "Additional Details & Limits" disclosure.
  - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape` or `/impeccable simplify`

- **[P2] Search Input Placeholder Contrast Below WCAG AA**
  - **Why it matters:** The placeholder text `#71717b` on `#0a0c12` in the perks search bar measures 4.0:1, failing the WCAG AA minimum 4.5:1 contrast requirement for accessibility.
  - **Fix:** Elevate placeholder color to `#94a3b8` (Slate-400) or `#a1a1aa`.
  - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

- **[P2] Jittery Bounce Easing on Urgent Payment Alerts**
  - **Why it matters:** `animate-bounce` in `CreditCardItem.tsx:209` and `WalletStack.tsx:432` introduces visual agitation and violates real-world deceleration physics.
  - **Fix:** Replace `animate-bounce` with a smooth, subtle exponential pulse (`animate-pulse`) or static high-contrast rose pill.
  - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

- **[P2] Missing Global Keyboard Accelerators for Power Users**
  - **Why it matters:** Users doing quick float lookups before checkout cannot jump to search or switch tabs without leaving the keyboard.
  - **Fix:** Implement `/` or `Ctrl+K` to focus the perk search bar, `1-3` to switch views, and `Escape` to clear.
  - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

- **[P3] Off-Scale Typography Drift in Modal Dialogs**
  - **Why it matters:** Secondary descriptions in `CardDetailModal.tsx` and `SettingsModal.tsx` drift into ad-hoc sizes (`text-[10px]`, `text-[11px]`) instead of sticking to the canonical `DESIGN.md` scale (`text-xs` / 12px).
  - **Fix:** Standardize non-card microcopy to `text-xs font-medium text-slate-400`.
  - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`
